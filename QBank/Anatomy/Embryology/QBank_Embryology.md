# Embryology QBank

## Q1. Pharyngeal Arch Arteries and Their Derivatives
**Question ID:** Q1785414690457
**Question:** During embryonic development, six pairs of pharyngeal arch arteries develop, though the 5th arch typically regresses. Which of the following pharyngeal arches are correctly matched with their main arterial derivatives?
1. 1st arch — Maxillary artery
2. 2nd arch — Stapedial & Hyoid artery
3. 3rd arch — Arch of aorta & right subclavian artery
4. 4th arch — Arch of aorta (left) & proximal right subclavian artery

- A) 1 and 3 only
- B) 1, 2, 4
- C) 2, 3, 4
- D) 1, 2, 3, 4

**Answer:** B) 1, 2, 4

**Explanation:**
During embryonic development, six pairs of pharyngeal arch arteries develop, though the 5th arch typically regresses. Each arch gives rise to specific arterial structures:

1. **1st arch → Maxillary artery (Correct):**
The 1st pharyngeal arch artery largely regresses, but its remnant forms the **maxillary artery** (terminal branch of external carotid artery). This vessel supplies structures derived from the 1st arch, including muscles of mastication and midface structures.

2. **2nd arch → Stapedial & Hyoid artery (Correct):**
The 2nd pharyngeal arch artery also regresses, leaving behind the **stapedial artery** (supplies stapes and middle ear) and **hyoid artery**.

3. **3rd arch → Common Carotid & proximal Internal Carotid Artery (Incorrect in statement 3):**
The 3rd arch artery forms the **common carotid artery** and the proximal part of the **internal carotid artery** (not the aorta/subclavian).

4. **4th arch → Arch of Aorta & Right Subclavian Artery (Correct):**
The 4th arch artery forms the **arch of aorta** on the left side and the proximal segment of the **right subclavian artery** on the right side.

5. **6th arch → Pulmonary arteries & Ductus Arteriosus:**
Forms the proximal pulmonary arteries and left ductus arteriosus (*Ligamentum arteriosum*).

**Take Home Message:** 
- 1st arch: Maxillary artery
- 2nd arch: Stapedial & Hyoid artery
- 3rd arch: Common Carotid & Internal Carotid artery
- 4th arch: Arch of Aorta (left) & Right Subclavian artery (right)
- 6th arch: Pulmonary arteries & Ductus arteriosus
