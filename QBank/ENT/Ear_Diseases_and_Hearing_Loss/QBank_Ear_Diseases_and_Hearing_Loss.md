# Ear Diseases and Hearing Loss QBank

## Q1. Vestibular System Function and Caloric Testing (COWS Mnemonic)
**Question ID:** Q5789001
**Question:** A 35-year-old male presents with episodes of vertigo and balance disturbance. During vestibular function evaluation, caloric testing is performed by irrigating the left external auditory canal with cold water. Which of the following is the expected normal nystagmus response?
- A) Fast component to the left, slow component to the right
- B) Fast component to the right, slow component to the left
- C) Fast component directed upwards, slow component downwards
- D) Rotary nystagmus with fast component to the left

**Answer:** B) Fast component to the right, slow component to the left

**Explanation:**
- **Caloric Testing Physiology:** Caloric irrigation of the external auditory meatus creates convection currents in the endolymph of the horizontal semicircular canal.
- **COWS Mnemonic:** **C**old = **O**pposite, **W**arm = **S**ame.
- **Mechanism:** Cold water irrigation inhibits the vestibular apparatus on the irrigated side, producing a slow conjugate movement of the eyes toward the tested (left) ear (slow component). The brainstem responds with a quick corrective saccade (fast component) to the **opposite (right) side**.
- **Nystagmus Naming Convention:** Nystagmus is conventionally named according to the direction of its **fast component**. Therefore, cold water in the left ear induces **right-beating nystagmus** (fast phase to the right).

**Take Home Message:** Caloric testing response follows the COWS mnemonic (Cold-Opposite, Warm-Same). Cold water in one ear produces nystagmus with its fast component directed to the opposite side, while warm water produces fast-phase nystagmus to the same side.
