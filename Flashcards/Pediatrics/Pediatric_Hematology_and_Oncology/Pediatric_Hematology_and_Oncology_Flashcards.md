# Pediatric Hematology and Oncology Flashcards

What is the preferred initial treatment for a pediatric patient with Immune Thrombocytopenic Purpura (ITP) presenting with severe thrombocytopenia (< 10,000/µL)?
?
**Intravenous Immunoglobulin (IVIG)** (saturates macrophage Fc receptors, rapidly increasing platelet count within 24–48 hours).
