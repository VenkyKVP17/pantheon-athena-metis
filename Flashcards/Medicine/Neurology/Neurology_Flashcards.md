What is the classic presentation of Subarachnoid Hemorrhage (SAH) and what is the key diagnostic management if initial NCCT is normal?
?
- **Classic Presentation:** Sudden onset severe ("thunderclap") headache, vomiting, neck stiffness (meningismus), and acute hydrocephalus.
- **Key Diagnostic Point:** Initial NCCT is normal in **~10% of cases**. If clinical suspicion of SAH remains high, perform a **Lumbar Puncture** (LP) to check for **xanthochromia**.
