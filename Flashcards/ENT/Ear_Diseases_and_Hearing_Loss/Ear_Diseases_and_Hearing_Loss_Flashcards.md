What is the expected nystagmus response during caloric testing of the vestibular system, and what mnemonic is used?
?
- **Response:** 
  - **Cold water irrigation:** Produces nystagmus with the fast phase to the **Opposite** side (slow phase to the same side).
  - **Warm water irrigation:** Produces nystagmus with the fast phase to the **Same** side.
- **Mnemonic:** **COWS** (**C**old = **O**pposite, **W**arm = **S**ame).
- **Naming Rule:** Nystagmus is named after the direction of its **fast component**.
