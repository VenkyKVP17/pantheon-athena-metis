# 📋 Carotid Body Tumor (Chemodectoma) Master Criteria Breakdown

What physical sign is characteristic of Carotid Body Tumor?
?
**Fontaine (Fountain) sign:** The neck swelling is mobile side-to-side (laterally) but fixed vertically (due to attachment at carotid bifurcation).
<!-- QBank ID: Q9836796 -->

What radiological sign on CT Angiogram / Angiography is diagnostic of Carotid Body Tumor?
?
**Lyre Sign** (splaying of the Internal Carotid Artery and External Carotid Artery at the bifurcation).
<!-- QBank ID: Q9836796 -->

What investigation is STRICTLY CONTRAINDICATED in a suspected Carotid Body Tumor?
?
**FNAC / Biopsy** (high risk of catastrophic bleeding and hematoma).
<!-- QBank ID: Q9836796 -->

Which cranial nerve is most commonly injured during surgical excision of a Carotid Body Tumor?
?
**Hypoglossal Nerve (CN XII)**.
<!-- QBank ID: Q9836796 -->

What is the main environmental risk factor for Carotid Body Tumors?
?
**Chronic hypoxia / High altitude living** (causes chemoreceptor hyperplasia).
