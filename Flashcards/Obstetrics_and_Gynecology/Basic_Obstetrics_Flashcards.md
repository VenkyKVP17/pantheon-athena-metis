# Basic Obstetrics & Fetal Physiology Flashcards

Which vessel carries the most highly oxygenated blood in the fetus, and what is its oxygen saturation?
?
**Umbilical Vein** (~80% O2 saturation).
<!-- QBank ID: Q4183116 -->

What percentage of right ventricular output is shunted away from the lungs across the ductus arteriosus into the descending aorta?
?
**~90%** (lungs receive only 8 to 10% of RV output due to high PVR).
<!-- QBank ID: Q4183116 -->

Which fetal shunt streams well-oxygenated umbilical venous blood directly into the IVC, bypassing hepatic sinusoids?
?
**Ductus Venosus**.
<!-- QBank ID: Q4183116 -->

Which decidual region forms the maternal component of the placenta?
?
**Decidua Basalis**.
<!-- QBank ID: Q4096410 -->

Which layer of the decidua serves as the natural cleavage plane along which the placenta shears off during the 3rd stage of labor?
?
**Middle Spongy Zone** (loose, full of dilated glands).
<!-- QBank ID: Q4096410 -->

The fusion of which two decidual regions obliterates the endometrial cavity by 10 to 12 weeks of gestation?
?
**Decidua Capsularis** and **Decidua Parietalis (Vera)**.
<!-- QBank ID: Q4096410 -->

At what gestational age does the oogonial pool peak in the female fetus, and how many oogonia are present?
?
**20th week of intrauterine life**; peaks at **~7 million oogonia**.
<!-- QBank ID: Q6165033 -->

At what stage of meiosis are primary oocytes arrested from fetal life until puberty?
?
**Prophase I (Diplotene stage)**.
<!-- QBank ID: Q6165033 -->

At what stage of meiosis is the ovulated secondary oocyte arrested, and what triggers completion of meiosis II?
?
Arrested in **Metaphase II**; completed **ONLY upon fertilization** by a sperm.
<!-- QBank ID: Q6165033 -->

How many functional gametes are produced from 1 primary spermatocyte vs 1 primary oocyte?
?
- **1 Primary Spermatocyte:** **4 functional spermatozoa**
- **1 Primary Oocyte:** **1 functional ovum + 3 polar bodies**
