# Endocrine Physiology Flashcards

Why is the uterus classified as a hormone target organ rather than an endocrine gland?
?
The **uterus** (composed of endometrium, myometrium, and perimetrium) responds to circulating hormones (**estrogen** and **progesterone**) for implantation and pregnancy, but **does NOT secrete hormones into the bloodstream** or possess endocrine function itself.
