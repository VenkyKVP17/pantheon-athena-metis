# General Physiology and Cell Flashcards

What is the exact stoichiometry and direction of ion transport by the Na+/K+ ATPase pump per ATP hydrolyzed?
?
The **Na+/K+ ATPase pump** hydrolyzes 1 ATP to transport **3 Na+ ions OUT** of the cell and **2 K+ ions IN** to the cell, maintaining osmotic balance and resting membrane potential.

Which transport mechanism couples the energy of the sodium electrochemical gradient to move glucose into enterocytes against its concentration gradient?
?
**SGLT1 (Sodium-Glucose Cotransporter 1)** utilizes **secondary active transport (symport)** powered by the electrochemical Na+ gradient (established by Na+/K+ ATPase) to transport glucose across the apical enterocyte membrane.
