# Neuroanatomy Flashcards

Which structure forms the medial boundary of the anterior horns of the lateral ventricles?
?
**Septum pellucidum** (a thin vertical membrane consisting of two laminae enclosing the cavum septi pellucidi / fifth ventricle).

Which cortical area controls visual scanning (conjugated saccadic eye movements)?
?
**Frontal Eye Field (FEF)** located in **Brodmann Area 8**.

Which cerebellar peduncle is the largest and connects the pons to the cerebellum?
?
**Middle cerebellar peduncle** (brachium pontis), formed by transverse pontine fibers (Superior connects midbrain; Inferior connects medulla).
