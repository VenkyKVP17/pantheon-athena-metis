# Lower Limb Flashcards

Which gluteal muscle acts as the chief extensor of the hip joint while also assisting in knee extension via its insertion into the iliotibial tract?
?
**Gluteus maximus**
Takeaway: Gluteus maximus originates behind the posterior gluteal line (Area A), sacrum, and coccyx; 3/4 of its fibers insert into the IT band to assist in knee extension.

Match the foot deformities (Equinus, Planus, Cavus, Calcaneus) with their anatomical descriptions.
?
**A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4**
- **Equinus:** Foot in plantar flexion
- **Planus:** Longitudinal arch flattened (flat foot)
- **Cavus:** Longitudinal arch extended dorsally (high arch)
- **Calcaneus:** Foot in dorsiflexion
