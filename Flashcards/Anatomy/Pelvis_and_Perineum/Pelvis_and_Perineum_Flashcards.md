# Pelvis and Perineum Flashcards

Which structures cross over the pelvic surface of the ischial spine / sacrospinous ligament to enter the lesser sciatic foramen, and which muscle tendon is NOT related to it?
?
- **Related structures (PIN + Ischiococcygeus):** **P**udendal nerve, Internal **P**udendal vessels, **N**erve to obturator internus, and **Ischiococcygeus (Coccygeus)** muscle.
- **Unrelated structure:** **Tendon of obturator externus** (passes behind the neck of femur to insert into the trochanteric fossa).
