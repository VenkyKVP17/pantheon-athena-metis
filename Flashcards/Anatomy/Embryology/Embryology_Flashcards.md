What are the major arterial derivatives of the 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, and 6th pharyngeal arches in embryonic development?
?
- **1st Arch:** Maxillary artery
- **2nd Arch:** Stapedial & Hyoid arteries
- **3rd Arch:** Common Carotid artery & proximal Internal Carotid artery
- **4th Arch:** Arch of Aorta (left) & Right Subclavian artery (right)
- **6th Arch:** Pulmonary arteries & Ductus arteriosus (left)
