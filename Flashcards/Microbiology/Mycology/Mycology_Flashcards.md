Which stain is least useful for identifying Pneumocystis jirovecii on lung biopsy in a post-transplant patient, and what are the preferred diagnostic stains?
?
- **Least Useful:** **Congo red** (detects **amyloid deposits** showing apple-green birefringence under polarized light).
- **Preferred Stains:** **Gomori Methenamine Silver (GMS - gold standard)** and **Periodic Acid-Schiff (PAS)** (stain cyst wall of *Pneumocystis jirovecii*).
